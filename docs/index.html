GitHub in 3D

Built from my public GitHub data. Hover a bar or a building to grow it, drag to turn the scene, click to open what it stands for.

01 Contribution skyline

One bar per day of the past year, coloured like GitHub's calendar. Click a bar to see that day's activity.

02 Repo city

One building per public repository: taller means more commits, greener roofs mean busier repositories. Click a building to open it.
